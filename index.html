Welcome to Twin Cities Animal Rescue

Twin Cities Animal Rescue is a community-focused nonprofit dedicated to helping animals in need. Our mission is to connect rescued animals with caring families while encouraging responsible pet ownership, fostering, and volunteer involvement.

Our Community Impact

Every adoption, foster placement, and volunteer contribution helps improve the lives of animals in our community.

The following figures are illustrative examples created for this student website project and do not represent verified organizational statistics.

  • 120 animals assisted through rescue programs.
  • 45 animals placed in temporary foster homes.
  • 80 community volunteers supporting rescue activities.
