Our Story

Twin Cities Animal Rescue is a community-focused nonprofit dedicated to improving the lives of animals in need. Our organization brings together compassionate volunteers, foster families, and community supporters who share a commitment to animal welfare.

We believe that every animal deserves safety, proper care, and an opportunity to find a loving home. Through adoption assistance, foster programs, and community outreach, we work to create positive outcomes for rescued animals and the people who welcome them into their lives.

Our Mission

Our mission is to support rescued animals by connecting them with responsible adopters, providing temporary foster care opportunities, and encouraging community involvement in animal welfare.

Our Core Values

Compassion

We believe every animal deserves kindness, patience, and respectful treatment regardless of its background or circumstances.

Responsibility

We encourage responsible pet ownership and thoughtful adoption decisions that support the long-term well-being of animals and their families.

Community

We recognize that successful animal rescue depends on cooperation among volunteers, foster families, adopters, and local supporters.

Education

We support community education about animal care, adoption preparation, and the importance of providing safe environments for pets.

Our Community Partners

Community partnerships help make animal rescue possible. We appreciate the contributions of individuals and organizations that support animal welfare in the Twin Cities area.

Partners and Supporters

  • Local Veterinary Professionals: Support animal health and wellness through veterinary services and guidance.
  • Foster Families: Provide temporary homes and daily care for animals awaiting adoption.
  • Community Volunteers: Assist with animal care, adoption events, and outreach activities.
  • Local Businesses and Donors: Help support rescue efforts through supplies, donations, and community awareness.

These examples represent the types of community support that strengthen animal rescue programs.
