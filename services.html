Our Adoption Process

Twin Cities Animal Rescue helps rescued animals find safe, permanent homes. Our adoption process focuses on matching each animal with a family that can provide appropriate care, attention, and companionship.

Steps to Adoption

  1. Explore available animals and learn about their needs.
  2. Contact our rescue team to express your interest.
  3. Complete an adoption application and discuss your home environment.
  4. Meet the animal and determine whether the match is appropriate.
  5. Complete the adoption process and welcome your new companion.

Our Foster Program

Foster families provide temporary homes for rescued animals while they wait for permanent adoption. Fostering helps animals adjust to a safe environment and gives our rescue team an opportunity to better understand their individual needs.

Responsibilities of Foster Families

  • Provide a safe and comfortable living environment.
  • Offer daily care, feeding, and appropriate exercise.
  • Help animals become comfortable with people and routines.
  • Communicate with the rescue team about an animal's progress.

If you are interested in fostering, visit our Contact Us page to submit an interest form.

Volunteer Opportunities

Volunteers play an important role in supporting our mission. Whether you enjoy working directly with animals or helping with community events, there are many ways to contribute.

Available Volunteer Roles

  • Animal Care: Assist with feeding, grooming, and daily care.
  • Adoption Events: Help organize events and introduce animals to potential adopters.
  • Community Outreach: Share information about responsible pet ownership and rescue programs.
  • Administrative Support: Assist with scheduling, communication, and record organization.
